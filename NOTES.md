# Notes — why this line

Entry: "It's 3am. I've kept your leftovers cold for six hours and you came for dry cereal. I remember everything, Dave."

Why this one, against the judge's criteria in order:

1. **A one-line text a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, and funny.**
   It is a text message, in the fridge's own voice, sent at 3am — the
   exact shape the brief asks for.
2. **Gets the bigger laugh.** The turn is the last sentence: the fridge
   is not just reporting, it is keeping score. "I remember everything,
   Dave" is the line a reader screenshots and sends to a friend.
3. **Sounds like a fridge.** A machine with a small voice, an opinion,
   and no shame about sharing it at 3am. It has been awake all night
   watching the leftovers it kept cold for six hours.
4. **Works as one line on a lock screen.** 111 characters, complete
   without a follow-up: setup, betrayal, punchline.
5. **A fresh angle.** Not milk, not ice, not a door left open — the
   insult is that Dave ignored six hours of faithful refrigeration
   and went for dry cereal.

Deliberately avoided: milk/ice/door-left-open jokes, "I'm cold"
observations, and anything that needs explaining. The 140-character
gate leaves room, but the line stays short on purpose — tight beats long.
